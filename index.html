📖 How to Play
🧠 The Idea
Guess the secret word by meaning, not spelling. Each guess gets a similarity score from 0% to 100%.
🎮 Game Modes
🎭 Daily Riddle [Login required]
A riddle is shown. Solve it to find the answer word. 3 difficulties. One attempt per day. Solving builds your streak — giving up resets it.
🌊 Daily Theme [Login required]
A theme category is revealed. Guess the specific hidden word. One attempt per day. Builds streak.
♾️ Random Mode [No login needed]
Unlimited practice. No streak. Counts toward Total Solved.
🌡️ Similarity Score
🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 90%+Extremely close!
🔥🔥🔥🔥 75%+Very warm
🔥🔥🔥 60%+Warm
🔥🔥 40%+Lukewarm
🔥 20%+Cold
❄️ 0–19%Freezing
📜 Rules
✓Type any real word — see how close it is semantically to the answer
✓Guesses sorted by similarity, highest at the top
✓Up to 5 hints — letter count, first letter, category, definition, synonym
✓Daily modes: 1 attempt/day. Giving up reveals answer but breaks streak
✓Plurals auto-accepted — "dogs" counts as "dog"
✗Gibberish/unknown words rejected — type correctly!
